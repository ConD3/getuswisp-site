Privacy Policy

Last updated August 30, 2026

The short version: we collect what you type into the contact form and nothing else. We do not run advertising trackers, we do not sell or share your information, and the free assessment reads only public DNS records, never your systems.

What we collect

  • What you send us. If you use the form, call, or email, we receive what you provide: your name, your firm's name, your domain, your email address and, if you give it, a phone number.
  • Correspondence. Emails and messages you send us, kept so we can answer you and keep a record of the engagement.
  • Assessment inputs. The domain name you give us, and the public DNS records published for it.

What the free assessment reads

The assessment queries your domain's public DNS records: the same records every mail server on the internet reads before accepting a message from you. It uses no credentials, accesses no systems, and installs nothing. We do not need, and do not ask for, access to your environment to produce it.

If you later engage us for a full Written Information Security Plan, any access to your Microsoft 365 environment is read-only, granted by you explicitly, revocable by you at any time, and described in writing before it is requested.

How we use it

To respond to you, to produce the assessment or documentation you asked for, and to keep our own business records. That is the whole list. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

Who else touches it

This is a small business running on ordinary tools, and honesty about them is part of the point of a privacy page:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and, like any web host, records standard server request data.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this page, which means your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads.
  • Formspree delivers the contact form to our inbox, if you choose to use the form rather than email or phone.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our email.

We do not currently run web analytics. If that changes, this page changes with it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and has no tracking pixels.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become engagements are kept while they are still live and then discarded. Records for firms we work with are kept for the duration of the engagement and afterward for as long as our own professional and tax record-keeping obligations require.

Your choices

Write to conde@getuswisp.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will do so unless we are required to keep it. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time and we will.

Confidentiality

Assessment findings about your firm belong to your firm. We do not publish them, do not name clients without written permission, and do not use one firm's findings as an example to another.

Children

This is a service sold to businesses. It is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.
